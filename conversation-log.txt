  the base text's colour and styles. The title field has its own ふ button.

Decisions worth knowing
- No third-party editor library. I built the editor on Android's own text
  field, and RubyTextView is a custom view rather than a library. That's
  what let the furigana keep the formatting of the text under it.
- Furigana is stored as {base|reading} text, so it survives copy/paste and
  works in any field. If only part of a word is formatted, the whole word
  gets that style once furigana is added.
- Language is required, category is optional. New entries default to the
  language you used last.
- Paste is plain text only, so formatting the app can't store never appears
  on screen.
